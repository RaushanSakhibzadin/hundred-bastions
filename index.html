Build the holding

Nothing here has a build timer. Buildings go up the moment you can afford them; what limits you is upkeep, which every structure draws against your ore production forever. Demolishing refunds in full, so rearranging your holding is free and you should do it often.

Choose a bastion

Every bastion is generated from its number, so the same number is the same holding for everyone. Scouting is free and always shown — the decision worth making is which warband to bring.

Marching with

Siege

While the siege runs

  • Tap the blue band — land the selected squad, all of it, in one gesture.
  • Long-press, or shift-tap — land a single body instead, to bait one defence.
  • 1–9 — switch squad. Space — pause. R — recall.
  • Rally — point the warband at somewhere specific. It biases target choice; it does not override it.
  • Recall — anything not engaged for two seconds walks off the field and can be landed again. A misread costs you seconds, not the attack.

About & Support

Hundred Bastions is free software. There is nothing to buy, no premium currency, no timers to skip, and no advertising. It is funded entirely by people who choose to donate, and it will keep working whether or not you do.

How it works

No art assets ship with this game. Every unit, turret and wall you see is drawn from a 32-bit number through a cellular automaton, a superellipse and a fractal emblem, then painted to a canvas. Every unit's statistics come out of one published equation that holds for all of them, which you can read in docs/BALANCE.md.

Your save

Everything is stored in this browser and nowhere else. There is no account and no server.
